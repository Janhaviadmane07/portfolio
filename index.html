Invention Summary & Abstract

A computer-implemented system for automated water tank level monitoring and pump control using Internet of Things technology. Real-time water level data is continuously acquired via contactless ultrasonic distance measurement and converted into percentage levels. An automated relay switching module controls pump activation when levels drop below low thresholds and deactivates before overflow, eliminating mechanical wear and extending equipment lifetime.

Contactless Ultrasonic Sensing Zero Mechanical Wear Automated Relay Switching Low Pump Cycling 49 Pages • 10 Claims
